  ## 1. Who is Scalby Fair and what do they do?

  Scalby Fair is one of the few remaining village fairs in the North of England. It is a week-long festival of entertainment and fun, culminating in a large village street fair with over 50 stalls, music, dancing and attractions for children. Scalby Fair is non-profit-making and run entirely by volunteers. Its aims are to foster community spirit by encouraging participation in varied activities and events, and to support local businesses. The Fair runs all the events during Fair Week and other events throughout the year, including the Scalby Walk each Easter Monday.

  ## 2. Who is collecting data?

  Data is collected by Scalby Fair committee members only: in person; through email and postal communication; through the Scalby Fair website; and through the Scalby Fair Facebook and Twitter accounts.

  ## 3. What data is being collected?

  Scalby Fair collects contact details for:

  - committee members;
  - past, current and potential stallholders; and
  - members of the public who want to know more about events run by Scalby Fair.

  ## 4. What is the legal basis for processing the data?

  The legal basis for holding and processing this data is that it is used solely to assist communication with committee members, past, current and potential stallholders, and members of the public who have expressed an interest in learning more about events run by Scalby Fair.

  ## 5. Will data be shared with any third parties?

  Data already in the public domain may be shared with third parties at the discretion of Scalby Fair, for example published telephone numbers and websites. Other data that is not in the public domain will only be shared with the express permission of the person or organisation to whom it relates.

  Payment information is entered directly into Stripe and is not stored by this website. Stripe processes payment and related personal data in accordance with its own privacy terms.

  ## 6. How will the information be used?

  The information held will be used to:

  - circulate information such as agendas and minutes to Scalby Fair Committee members;
  - contact potential stallholders about booking a stall at Scalby Fair;
  - record personal details required for Scalby Fair events such as the Scalby Walk;
  - process donations and event payments; and
  - contact members of the public about events arranged by Scalby Fair, where they have asked to receive those updates.

  ## 7. How long will the data be stored for?

  Data will be stored for as long as Scalby Fair determines there is a need, or possible need, to use it.

  Where an email address is held, the person will be contacted periodically and given the opportunity to ask for their details to be deleted.

  ## 8. What rights does the data subject have?

  Anyone for whom Scalby Fair holds data may ask for a copy of that data or request that it is corrected or deleted. Scalby Fair asks for 14 days’ notice to action a deletion request.

  ## 9. How can the data subject raise a complaint?

  Complaints can be raised by [contacting the Scalby Fair Committee Secretary](/contact) or by writing to Scalby Fair Committee, c/o The Nag’s Head, 35 High Street, Scalby, Scarborough, YO13 0PT.

  All complaints will be responded to in writing within 15 working days.
