content: |-
  Scalby Fair brings together residents, local organisations, performers, businesses and visitors for a programme of events throughout Fair Week, culminating in a lively village street fair with stalls, music, dancing, food and entertainment for all ages.

  The Fair exists to foster community spirit, encourage people to take part and support local businesses and good causes. Alongside Fair Week and Fair Day, the committee also organises the long-established Scalby Walk and other events throughout the year.

  Run on a not-for-profit basis, every part of the Fair depends on volunteers: the people who plan the programme, put up stalls, marshal events, welcome performers, prepare food and quietly solve hundreds of practical problems.
