The AGM of Scalby Fair took place on Wednesday 11 November at 6pm in the Plough, Scalby High Street.  The Committee was delighted that Scalby Fair Charity Walk at Easter and the fair in June were both very successful, bringing together our community and raising in excess of £5,000 for this year's nominated Charities. We would welcome any new members who would like to help us develop our plans for 2027.
