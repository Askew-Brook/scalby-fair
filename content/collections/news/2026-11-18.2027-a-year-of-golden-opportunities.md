The Scalby Fair Committee is already working on ways to make the Golden Anniversary of this unique event special. As one of the last remaining volunteer-run street fairs in the county, the 50 years milestone is very special. You might enjoy reading the history of the fair elsewhere on this site. 
As well as all of the traditional events, 2027 will see some innovation including "Pop Up" happenings throughout the year.
