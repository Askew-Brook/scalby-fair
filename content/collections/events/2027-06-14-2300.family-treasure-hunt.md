Fun for families, groups of friends—in fact, everyone. Follow clues through the village, work together and see familiar corners of Scalby in a new way.

The treasure hunt is one of Fair Week's enduring traditions. Historically, the winning team has set the following year's hunt, passing the challenge from one group of local clue-makers to the next.

Hot dogs will be on sale during the event.
