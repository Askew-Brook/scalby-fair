  Fair Day is the joyful culmination of more than a week of community events. In 2026, the theme is **The Circus**.

  A grand opening parade sets off from Yew Court at 11.45am before the programme begins across the Main Stage, Yew Court Live, the Challenge Arena, Demonstration Garden and Bouncing Mad Garden.

  Britain's Got Talent semi-finalist Nancy drums in the afternoon at midday. Visitors can also enjoy Magic Mike, Kevin O'Connor's Irish Dancers, Hatton's Performing Arts, the Scalby Songbirds, Korali Ukrainian Choir, martial arts, birds of prey, classic games, craft demonstrations, more than eighty stalls and plenty of food.
