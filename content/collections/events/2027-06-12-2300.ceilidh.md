Scalby Fair Week 2026 begins with a traditional ceilidh led by the wonderful band **The Whippets**.

Everyone is welcome, whether you know the dances already or are trying a ceilidh for the first time. The caller will guide the room through each dance, making this a warm and lively start to the week.

Doors open at 7pm. Please bring your own drinks and cold food, as refreshments are not available at the Village Hall.
